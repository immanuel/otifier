Verification Code

Two things that matter

  • 01

    Stays on your Mac

    No network calls. No telemetry. No analytics. Codes are matched and copied locally — they never leave your machine.

  • 02

    Any mirrored notification

    iMessage from your iPhone, email previews, app banners — anything macOS surfaces as a notification, Otifier reads.

How it works

  1. 1iPhone receives a code via SMS, email or app push.
  2. 2macOS mirrors the notification to your menu bar.
  3. 3Otifier reads the banner and copies the code.
  4. 4You hit ⌘V wherever you needed it.
