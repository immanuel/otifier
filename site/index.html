Get started

  1. 01

    Mirror your iPhone notifications to your Mac

    Otifier reads codes from macOS notification banners, so iPhone notifications need to be forwarded to your Mac first. See Apple’s setup guide.

  2. 02

    Install Otifier

    Download the DMG, open it, and drag Otifier to your Applications folder.

  3. 03

    Grant Accessibility permission

    Launch Otifier from Applications. macOS will prompt for Accessibility access — this is how Otifier reads notification banners. Approve it once and you’re done.

  4. 04

    Paste anywhere

    When a verification code arrives, Otifier copies it to your clipboard and shows a small confirmation. Hit ⌘V wherever you need it.
